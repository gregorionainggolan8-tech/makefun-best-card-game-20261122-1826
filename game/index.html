    Lantern Clash

    Duel of the Ember Isles

    An original card duel. Outwit Sable, Keeper of the Far Lantern, before your lantern burns out. Match suits to spark Resonance: the second card of a suit you play in one turn hits +2 harder.

    🔥 Flame 💧 Tide 🍃 Gale 🪨 Stone 🏮 Lantern

    30 life each · Energy grows each round · First lantern out loses

    How to play

    • Goal: reduce Sable's life from 30 to 0 before yours reaches 0.
    • Each round you gain embers (energy) and draw a card. Energy grows by 1 every round, up to 8.
    • Play a card by clicking it or pressing its number key (1–7). Each card costs embers, shown in the top-left gem.
    • Attack cards deal their power as damage. Block (🛡) absorbs damage until your next turn. Heal restores life.
    • Resonance: the second card of the same suit you play in one turn gains +2 power. Plan your hand around it.
    • End your turn with the button or Space — then Sable plays.
    • Keyboard: 1–7 play a card · Space/Enter end turn · R restart · M sound · H help.
